Nithya privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026

In short

Nithya has no accounts, no ads and no payments. Your puja settings, streak and city stay on your phone. We collect a small set of anonymous usage events to learn whether the app is helping people keep their daily puja; they carry no name, email, phone number or location.

What stays on your phone

Your language, deity, puja length, chant settings, reminder time, city and the days you completed a puja are stored only on your device. We never receive them.

Android may include this app data in your own Google backup if backup is turned on for your phone. That backup is between you and Google; we cannot read it.

Location

If you tap "Use my location", the app asks Android for your approximate location once, rounds it to about 1 km and uses it on your phone to work out sunrise and the panchang. It is saved only on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. The app never asks for precise or background location. You can pick a city from the list instead.

Reminders

The daily reminder is scheduled on your phone. No server is involved and nothing about it leaves your device. You can turn it off in Me or in Android settings.

Anonymous usage events

To improve the app we record events such as "puja started", "puja completed", "japa completed", your streak length and which settings you changed (for example chant speed or script). Each event carries a random ID created when the app is installed, which is not linked to you, your device identifiers or your Google account, plus the app version, operating system, device type and screen size.

Events are sent over an encrypted connection to PostHog, our analytics provider, which stores them in the United States. IP addresses are not used to work out your location, and we do not build profiles of people. We do not sell this data, share it for advertising or combine it with other data.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes the random ID. Events already received cannot be linked back to you, so we cannot find or delete them individually.

Children

Nithya is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on this page with a new date.
